What AI looks at

  • Your Google Business Profile: categories, hours, photos and reviews
  • Doctor names, specialties and languages on your own website
  • Insurance networks you accept, stated clearly
  • Consistent details on DHA-related listings and medical directories
  • What patients write in reviews: “gentle”, “on time”, “Arabic-speaking”

What we fix

  • A verified fact page for the clinic and each doctor, in Arabic and English
  • Structured data that marks you as a medical business with opening hours and specialties
  • Answer pages for the exact questions where competitors are named
  • A review request routine that asks patients to mention what they came for
  • A WhatsApp assistant that answers and books appointments in both languages

Clinics

Is it allowed to market a clinic this way in the UAE?

Yes, as long as the content is factual and follows the health authority's advertising rules. We work only with accurate information you approve, and we never make medical claims on your behalf.

Do you need patient data?

No. The AI Check and the fixes use public information about your clinic only.

Which specialties do you work with?

Dental, dermatology, paediatrics, physiotherapy, general practice and other outpatient clinics.

How fast can a clinic see a change?

The fixes are in place within 7 days. AI assistants then take weeks to pick them up, so we measure over 90 days.
