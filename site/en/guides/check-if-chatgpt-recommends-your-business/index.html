How to check if ChatGPT recommends your business

A 20-minute test you can run yourself: which questions to ask, which assistants to try, what to write down, and how to read the result.

Published 2026-11-13

You do not need a tool to find out whether AI assistants name your business. You need a list of questions, twenty minutes and a notebook. Here is the method we use, simplified.

1. Write the questions your customers actually ask

Think about the moment before someone chooses you. For a café in Al Quoz it is “quiet café to work from near Al Quoz” or “best pour-over in Dubai”. For a clinic it is “dentist for children in Jumeirah”. Write ten questions. Use the words customers use, including questions in Arabic if your customers write in Arabic.

2. Ask more than one assistant

Ask the same ten questions to ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity and Google’s AI answers. They read different sources, so they often disagree. Open a fresh chat each time, so earlier questions do not influence the answer.

3. Write down what you see

  • The date and the assistant
  • The exact question
  • Which businesses were named, in what order
  • Whether yours was named, and whether the facts about you were right (hours, area, prices)

4. Read the result carefully

Count how many answers named you out of how many you read. That count is more useful than any rank, because assistants do not keep a fixed ranking and the same question can give a different answer tomorrow. Pay attention to wrong facts: an assistant that names you with the wrong hours is telling you which page it read.

5. Repeat every week

One check is a snapshot. The trend over several weeks is the real number. If you repeat the same questions weekly and keep the log, you will see whether the changes you make are working.

What to do when you are not named

Usually the cause is simple: your hours, area, prices or services are not written as text on a page the assistant can read, or your name is written differently in different places. Our 15-point checklist covers the fixes. If you would rather have a person do the whole check and send you a report, request your free Tawsiya Score.

No method can promise that an assistant will recommend you. What you can do is make it easy for them to read and trust you, and measure honestly.
