The 15-point AI readiness checklist for a UAE business

The 15 checks behind the Tawsiya Score, grouped and explained, with the simple fix for each.

Published 2026-11-13

AI assistants and search engines answer from what they can read. If the facts about your business are missing, scattered or contradictory, they will use someone else’s. These are the 15 checks behind the Tawsiya Score, in plain words. Pass all fifteen and the page is ready to be read; whether an assistant then names you is still up to the assistant.

Your website

  • Loads securely and works on a phone. Fix: use HTTPS and test on a real phone.
  • Says where you are and what area you serve, in text. Fix: write it on a page, not only in a map or image.
  • States opening hours in text. Fix: a line of text, kept up to date.
  • States phone or WhatsApp and email in text. Fix: write them as text, not an image.
  • Lists services or products in words. Fix: a plain list with a sentence about each.
  • States prices or price ranges. Fix: even a range such as “from AED 150” answers “how much”.
  • States the languages you serve and has real Arabic pages. Fix: write the Arabic pages; do not rely on automatic translation.
  • Has pages that answer customer questions. Fix: one short page per real question.
  • Has an About page that says who you are. Fix: who, since when, and why customers trust you.
  • Allows crawlers and has a sitemap. Fix: check robots.txt does not block everything.

Your profiles and listings

  • Business name is identical everywhere. Fix: choose one spelling, Arabic and English, and use it on your site, Google and social profiles.
  • Google Business Profile is claimed and complete. Fix: fill every field, add photos, keep hours current.
  • Name, address and phone match on the main directories. Fix: correct the outliers.
  • Recent reviews exist and are answered. Fix: ask happy customers, reply to every review.

Your machine-readable layer

  • Structured data (schema.org LocalBusiness) describes the business. Fix: add it once to the site so a machine can read the facts without guessing.

Most businesses fail five to eight of these on their first check, usually the text-based ones: hours, prices and services that live in images or PDFs. They are the cheapest to fix. Request your free Tawsiya Score and we will show which of the 15 pass and which do not, with a reason for each.
