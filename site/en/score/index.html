Two things we measure

Readiness

15 published checks of your website and profiles: can AI and Google read, trust and quote you? Each check passes or fails, with a reason.

AI answer share

We ask customer questions to four assistants, with web search on, on a stated date and count how often your business is named. We list the questions and the assistants in your report.

The 15 checks

Every check is public. This is exactly what we look at.

  1. The website loads securely and works on a phoneAI and search crawlers skip sites that fail to load or warn visitors.
  2. Your business name is written the same on your site, Google and social profilesDifferent spellings split your reputation into several unrelated entities.
  3. A page says in plain text where you are and what area you serveAssistants answer “near me” questions only from places they can read.
  4. Opening hours are written as text, not only in an imageText can be read and quoted; text inside images usually cannot.
  5. Phone or WhatsApp and email are written as textA customer cannot be sent to you if the assistant cannot find how to reach you.
  6. Your services or products are listed in wordsThis is what assistants match to the customer’s question.
  7. Prices or price ranges are statedMany questions are “how much”; no answer on your site means an answer from someone else’s.
  8. The languages you serve are stated, with real Arabic pagesArabic questions are answered from Arabic pages.
  9. Pages answer the questions customers actually askAnswer pages are what assistants quote.
  10. Structured data (schema.org) describes the businessA machine-readable summary removes guesswork.
  11. The Google Business Profile is claimed and completeGoogle and several assistants rely on it for local facts.
  12. Name, address and phone match on the main directoriesMatching listings are how assistants confirm a business is real.
  13. Recent reviews exist and are answeredReviews are a trust signal and a source of the words customers use.
  14. An About page tells who you are and why customers should trust youAssistants describe businesses from what they can read about them.
  15. Crawlers are allowed in, and a sitemap existsA site that blocks crawlers cannot be recommended.
