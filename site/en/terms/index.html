The free AI Check

The AI Check is a snapshot of how AI assistants answered a set of questions on the date of the check. AI answers change over time and with wording, so the report is information, not a promise of future results. The check is free and creates no obligation.

Paid plans

Paid plans are prepaid. Each payment, made by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay through Ziina, covers one period (a month or a year) from the date of payment, or extends your current period if you renew early. Nothing renews or is charged automatically; we email you before your plan ends. If you change to another plan, the unused part of your current paid period is credited against the new price. Payments are not refundable for partial periods unless the law requires it. If a plan is not renewed, the account moves to the free plan after a 3-day grace period and your history is kept. The price is always shown before you pay.
