Questions, answered plainly.

What does Tawsiya do?

We check what ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Perplexity say when your customers ask for a business like yours. Then we fix what stops them from naming you: your website, Google profile, listings, reviews and business facts. Every week we ask again, so you can see what changed.

Is the free report really free?

Yes. No card and no account. Paste your website and in a few minutes you get your score out of 100, the real answers, the competitors named instead of you, and a list of fixes.

Do you guarantee AI will recommend me?

No, and nobody honest can. Each assistant decides on its own and answers change over time. What we stand behind is the work: clear fixes, done or explained, and the same questions asked again every week so progress is measured, not guessed.

How soon will I see a difference?

Website and profile fixes can show within a few weeks. Listings and reviews usually take one to three months. Your weekly scan shows each change as it happens.

What is the difference between Track and Grow?

Track (AED 199 a month) measures you every week and gives you a step-by-step plan you carry out yourself. Grow (AED 799 a month) is done for you: our team makes the fixes, tracks more questions and competitors, and reviews progress with you on WhatsApp every month.

Will I be charged automatically?

No. Plans are paid one month at a time through Ziina and never renew by themselves. We remind you 7 days and 1 day before your month ends, and you decide whether to continue.

Do you work in Arabic and English?

Yes. We ask AI the way your customers do, in Arabic and in English, and your report and dashboard work in both languages.

Which businesses is it for?

Any UAE business that customers look for: clinics, restaurants and cafés, real estate, salons and spas, and professional services. If people ask AI for the best place like yours in Dubai, it is for you.

What happens to my data?

We read only your public website and public listings. Your report is a private link, and we never sell or share your data.
