Brand story

Aurelle takes its name from the aureole, the soft halo of light around something precious. It was conceived for women in the Gulf who want results they can trust and a routine that feels like a pause, not a chore: few products, proven actives, and packaging worth keeping on the shelf.

Audience

  • Women 28–45 in the UAE and KSA
  • Clean-beauty buyers moving to premium
  • Gift buyers for weddings and Eid

Products & services

  • Renewal serum
  • Barrier cream
  • Hydrating mist
  • The Ritual gift set

Brand in use

Brand in use

Website concept

A slow, editorial e-commerce site: one product per screen, ingredient stories, and a 60-second skin quiz that recommends a ritual.

Marketing direction

Launch through dermatologist-led content and a limited first edition sold by waitlist, then quarterly ritual drops.

Social media direction

Ivory and champagne grids, close-up texture shots, Arabic calligraphy moments for Ramadan and Eid.

Business & growth

  • Expansion to KSA
